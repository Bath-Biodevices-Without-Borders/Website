Cookie policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This site doesn't use cookies. That's why you haven't seen a cookie banner.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage and tracking pixels, work in a similar way. Under UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations), websites must ask for your consent before using any that aren't strictly necessary.

What this site uses

None. We don't set cookies, use browser storage, or load analytics, advertising, social media or video embeds. Everything the site needs, including its fonts, is served from our own site.

When we checked the site on 28 October 2026, no cookies or similar technologies were set.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as the sources for our statistics and team members' LinkedIn and Indeed profiles. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which apply once you visit them.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that needs cookies, we'll ask for your consent first, using a banner where you can say no as easily as yes, and we'll update this page.
