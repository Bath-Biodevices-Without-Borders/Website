Cookie policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This site doesn't use cookies. That's why you haven't seen a cookie banner. The only thing we store in your browser is a sign-in token, and only if you are a team member and you sign in to our team dashboard.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage and tracking pixels, work in a similar way. Under UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations), websites must ask for your consent before using any that aren't strictly necessary.

What this site uses

We don't set cookies, or load analytics, advertising, social media or video embeds. Everything the site needs, including its fonts, is served from our own site.

The one exception is our team dashboard (dashboard.html), which only Bath Biodevices team members can sign in to. When a team member signs in, the dashboard stores a sign-in token in browser local storage (named sb-cvjyymfzdnaucjahufzc-auth-token) so they stay signed in. Signing out removes it. This storage is strictly necessary for a service the team member asked for, so it doesn't need consent. Nothing is stored for visitors who don't sign in.

When we checked the site on 19 November 2026, no cookies or similar technologies were set for visitors who don't sign in to the team dashboard.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as the sources for our statistics and team members' LinkedIn and Indeed profiles. Those sites have their own cookie policies, which apply once you visit them.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that needs cookies, we'll ask for your consent first, using a banner where you can say no as easily as yes, and we'll update this page.
